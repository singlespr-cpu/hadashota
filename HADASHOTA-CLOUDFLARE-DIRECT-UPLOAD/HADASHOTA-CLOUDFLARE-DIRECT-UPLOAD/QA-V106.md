# V106 — Working Important / Near-you Filters
- "רק חשוב" now visibly filters rendered feed cards by the site's importance score.
- Shows result count on the button and a clear status bar above the feed.
- "הצג הכל" restores the full feed.
- "מה קורה לידך" searches title, preview, description, location/city/region and clustered reports.
- Added common Israeli city aliases (e.g. תל אביב / ת"א / יפו / גוש דן).
- City filter shows an explicit result count or a clear no-significant-news message.
- Enter key submits city selection.
- Active filters are reapplied after background refreshes so they don't disappear every 30 seconds.
- Filters are mutually exclusive to avoid confusing hidden-card states.
