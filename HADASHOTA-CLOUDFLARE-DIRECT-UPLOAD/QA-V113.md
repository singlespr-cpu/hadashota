# V113 — Live Lead Refresh Fix

Root cause:
The anti-flicker lead lock could preserve state.currentLeadEntry after a fresh
source refresh. A full browser reload cleared that in-memory state, which is why
the new lead only appeared after a browser refresh.

Fix:
- Every forced source collection (manual, 30-second auto, resume) sets a one-shot
  forceLeadReevaluation flag.
- The anti-flicker lock is bypassed for that fresh snapshot only.
- Ordinary internal renders still keep the anti-flicker protection.
- Even if the overall item fingerprint did not change, renderLeadStory() is
  reevaluated after a successful background refresh because time/source
  eligibility can change.
- Manual refresh now awaits the actual news refresh and updates utilities after it.
- V112 instant paint and V111 image-context safeguards remain.
